How it works

The board, the coins, the pool.

TEK CITY is a 24-space board. Every space except START can hold one Pump.fun coin. Empty spaces stay blank until someone launches a coin there; then the coin's name and image fill the space.

Spins

  • Buy TEK CITY: every 500,000 TEK CITY you buy earns 1 free spin. Only real buys count: transactions your own wallet signed and paid for. Tokens sent to you from another wallet don't.
  • Pass START: every time your pawn passes or lands on START you get another free spin.
  • Before TEK CITY launches: everyone gets 1 free spin per 15-minute round.

The server rolls the die. Your browser only shows the result.

Launching a coin

Land on an empty space, pick a name, ticker and image, and choose your first buy. TEK CITY builds the Pump.fun launch transaction, you approve it in your wallet, and the space becomes your coin. Right after, you approve the creator-fee split: 80% of your coin's creator fees go to you, 20% to the community pool.

Growing and taking over

  • Grow: land on a coin and buy into it on Pump.fun. The amount is added to that space's total.
  • Take over: launch your own coin on a taken space with a first buy at least as large as the biggest single buy-in on that space. Your coin replaces it on the board. The old coin keeps trading on Pump.fun.

The community pool

  • Filled by 20% of the creator fees from every coin launched on the board.
  • Vault: land on the Vault space to win 60% of the pool, paid to your linked wallet.
  • Hourly: every hour, 20% of the pool is split across the top 25 holders by TEK CITY balance.

Linking pump.fun

If you use Phantom or another wallet on pump.fun, connect that wallet and your pump.fun profile shows up automatically. If you signed up on pump.fun with email or social login, paste your profile link and add the short code we give you to your pump.fun bio. Your TEK CITY buys from that wallet then count toward spins and holder rewards.
