Milestones

What each milestone unlocks

When the TEK CITY coin first reaches a market cap on pump.fun, that milestone unlocks for good and its reward runs automatically from the rewards wallet. Each one links to its transactions on Solana.

    Who counts as a holder

    Any player whose linked wallet or verified pump.fun profile holds at least 500,000 TEK CITY. Link yours on the board.
