How it works

Three moves.

01

Spin

Every 500,000 TEK CITY you buy earns a free spin. Passing START earns another. The server rolls the die, so nobody can fake a roll.

02

Launch or grow

Empty space: launch your coin on Pump.fun right from the board. Taken space: buy into that coin, or take the space with a bigger first buy.

03

Share the pool

20% of creator fees from every coin on the board go to the community pool. Land on the Vault to win most of it. Every hour, part of it is split across holders.

The rules

Plain numbers, enforced by the server.

No hidden mechanics. The server sets every roll, balance and payout. Every launch and buy links to its transaction on Solana.
